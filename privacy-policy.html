NutriReps Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

NutriReps ("the app") helps you plan workouts, track nutrition and log daily check-ins. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google or Apple we receive an account identifier, your name and your email address (Apple may give you a private relay address instead).
  • Profile and body data you enter. Age, gender, height, weight, activity level and goals, used to calculate your calorie and macro targets.
  • Fitness and nutrition data you enter. Workout programs and sessions, nutrition programs and food logs, custom foods and exercises, and daily and bi-weekly check-ins (for example weight, sleep, water, hunger level).
  • Camera. The camera is used only to scan food barcodes. Images are processed on your device and are not stored or uploaded. The scanned barcode number is sent to the Open Food Facts database to look up the product.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, the app can show local notifications such as rest-timer alerts.

The app does not contain advertising, does not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

How we use your information

Only to provide the app's features: signing you in, calculating your targets, saving your plans and logs, and showing your progress.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with the following service providers, who process it on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase Authentication, for sign-in.
  • MongoDB Atlas, for your profile and fitness data.
  • Railway, which hosts the app's server.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: More → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your profile, all fitness and nutrition data, and your sign-in account. You can also contact us at the address below and we will delete it for you.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our database.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Health disclaimer

NutriReps is a convenience and personal development tool only. We make no promise of any results. The calorie, protein, fat and carb values are general estimates, and using them, and any decision about nutrition or training, is your own responsibility. Always consult a qualified professional, such as a dietitian or medical professional, in every case and before any change.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
