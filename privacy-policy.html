NutriReps Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

NutriReps ("the app") helps you plan workouts, track nutrition and log daily check-ins. The app is operated by Dvir Mor Yosef, Israel ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who else receives it, and the choices and rights you have.

Who may use the app

The app is for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google or Apple we receive an account identifier, your name and your email address (Apple may give you a private relay address instead).
  • Profile and body data you enter. Age, gender, height, weight, body fat (optional), activity level and goals, used to calculate your calorie and macro targets.
  • Fitness and nutrition data you enter. Workout programs and sessions, nutrition programs and food logs, foods and exercises you create, and daily and bi-weekly check-ins (for example weight, sleep, water, hunger level).
  • Camera. The camera is used only to scan food barcodes. Images are processed on your device and are not stored or uploaded. The scanned barcode number is sent from our server to the Open Food Facts database to look up the product.
  • Exercise search. When you search the exercise library, the search text is sent from your device to wger.de, which also sees your device's IP address.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, the app can show local notifications such as rest-timer alerts.
  • Apple Health (optional, iPhone). If you turn on "Read steps from Apple Health" (More), the app reads only your daily step totals and saves them with your daily check-ins. It never writes to Apple Health and reads nothing else from it. Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared beyond the providers listed below. You can turn it off in the app, or remove access in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

The app has no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools, and does not track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Why we use your information, and on what basis

Only to provide the app's features: signing you in, calculating your targets, saving your plans and logs, and showing your progress.

Some of what you enter (such as weight, body fat and nutrition) is health-related. We process it only with your consent, which you give when you start using the app. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account (More → Delete Account). Without it the app cannot calculate your targets.

Who receives your information

Other users of the app cannot see your data. The foods you create stay private to you. Products you scan are saved as a personal copy for you. We do not share your data for advertising.

Your data is processed on our behalf by the following providers:

  • Google Firebase Authentication, for sign-in (including Sign in with Google), and Apple, for Sign in with Apple.
  • MongoDB Atlas, for your profile and fitness data (stored in Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Railway, which hosts the app's server (in Amsterdam, the Netherlands).
  • GitHub, which stores the encrypted nightly backups of the database (in the United States).

These providers process data on servers outside Israel, in Germany, the Netherlands and the United States, and may process it in other countries. By using the app you agree to this transfer. We use providers that apply their own security and data protection terms. Where law requires, we will disclose your data to authorities.

The services described above under barcode and exercise search (Open Food Facts and wger.de) receive only the barcode number or search text, not your account details.

Security

Data is sent between the app and our server over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Every request is checked against your sign-in, and you can only reach your own data. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it (you can also edit your profile in the app), ask for a copy, object to how it is used, or ask us to delete it. To make a request, write to the address below. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: More → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your profile, all fitness and nutrition data, and your sign-in account. If you cannot use the app, email us from the address you signed in with and write "Delete my account" in the subject, and we will delete it for you.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our database. We keep backup copies of the database to protect against data loss. Backups are encrypted, stored securely and each one is deleted within 35 days, so data you delete is also gone from the backups within 35 days.

Health disclaimer

NutriReps is a convenience and personal development tool only. We make no promise of any results. The calorie, protein, fat and carb values are general estimates, and using them, and any decision about nutrition or training, is your own responsibility. Always consult a qualified professional, such as a dietitian or medical professional, in every case and before any change.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above. For a significant change we will also tell you in the app.
